About

I am an AI Scientist at Qure.ai in Bangalore, where I work on lung-nodule analysis in chest CT. I completed my degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering, with a Minor in Data Science, at BITS Pilani, Goa Campus in July 2025.

My bachelor's thesis at CAMMA (University of Strasbourg, CNRS) adapted CLIP for multi-task surgical computer vision and was published at MICCAI 2025, where it was nominated for the Best Paper and Young Scientist Awards. Before that, I did a research internship at CSIR-CEERI on anomaly segmentation in baggage X-rays. I am currently also working on learned on-board compression of satellite SAR data with complex-valued networks.

Research Projects

Learned On-Board Compression of SAR Data with Complex-Valued Networks

May 2026 – Present · Mentor: Mr. Rupak Bose

A learned compression system for satellite SAR single-look complex (SLC) data: an on-board encoder maps each image to a fixed 2048-value code for downlink, and the ground station reconstructs amplitude and phase. Includes real- and complex-valued hierarchical autoencoders and a conditional flow-matching decoder.

Key result: 64× fewer values per image; the complex encoder uses half the parameters of the baseline (57M vs. 113M).

  • Autoencoders

Neural Datacube for Geospatial Data Discovery and Compression

Mentor: Mr. Rupak Bose

A NeRF-inspired neural architecture with a continuous spatiotemporal representation, enabling reconstruction and sampling at arbitrary spatial resolutions. Optimized for CUDA graph compatibility and deployed for edge inference with ONNX Runtime.

Analog Spiking Neural Network for Unsupervised Compression

Aug 2023 – May 2024 · Guide: Dr. Abhijit Pethe, BITS Pilani, Goa Campus

An unsupervised spiking network in which rate-coded inputs drive leaky integrate-and-fire neuron circuits through STDP-learning synapse circuits; the firing rates of ~100 output neurons form a compressed representation. Circuits designed in Cadence Virtuoso, network simulated in Brian2.

Key result: 94.76% accuracy on MNIST from 100 output rates (vs. 784 input pixels).

Selected Projects

Push-T Imitation Learning: Diffusion and Uncertainty-Aware Policies

Four policies built from scratch on a shared ResNet-18 + transformer backbone: an action-chunking transformer (ACT) baseline, a DDPM diffusion policy with DDIM sampling, and two uncertainty-aware policies (masked diffusion and a causally masked autoregressive Gaussian policy).

Setup: trained on 206 human demonstrations; robustness tested under three visual distribution shifts.

  • PyTorch

GitHub

Masked-Diffusion Speech Recognition (LLaDA-style)

A 15M-parameter masked-diffusion transformer decoder, trained from scratch on ~60 h of LibriSpeech, that conditions on frozen Whisper-base features and generates transcripts by iterative unmasking. Includes a CPU-only web demo.

Key result: WER on test-clean falls from 31.3% to 20.8% as decoding steps increase from 8 to 128.

  • PyTorch
  • Hugging Face
  • FastAPI

GitHub

3D Point Cloud Segmentation from Scratch

An end-to-end part and scene segmentation pipeline with no point-cloud libraries, implementing PointNet with input and feature T-Nets, plus an extension with heteroscedastic aleatoric uncertainty.

Key result: 0.807 instance mIoU on ShapeNetPart.
